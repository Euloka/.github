## Discover Bhutan, one experience at a time

Euloka is a platform for exploring what Bhutan has to offer: guided treks,
cultural workshops, local food, tshechu festivals and community events. It
brings travellers and locals together with the hosts, guides and organisers
behind each experience, all in one place.

### What you can do with Euloka

- **Explore experiences**: browse curated activities across Bhutan's dzongkhags,
  from Thimphu and Paro to Bumthang and beyond.
- **Follow events**: keep up with festivals, markets and gatherings as they
  happen.
- **Meet people**: connect with hosts, guides and the wider community through
  a shared feed.
